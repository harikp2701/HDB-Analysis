
# Resale Flat Price Dashboard Project

## Project Overview
This project involves creating two Tableau dashboards that analyze and visualize the resale prices of 4-room flats in Singapore. You will explore data trends from 2019 to 2023, focusing on both price trends and location-based insights.

## Data Files
- **Tableau Workbook (.twb):** Prakash_Hari_Prasad.twb
- **CSV File:** ResaleflatpricesbasedonregistrationdatefromJan2017onwards.csv

## Grading Criteria
1. **Data Visualizations and Annotations:** Answer all eight questions with appropriate visualizations and annotations.
2. **Visual Vocabulary and Design Principles:** Use suitable chart types, colors, and layouts to enhance readability and insights.
3. **Effective Communication with Tableau Data Story:** Utilize Tableau features such as Story Points, chart titles, and annotations to communicate findings.

## Dashboard Requirements

### Dashboard 1 - Price
Focuses on price trends and factors affecting the resale prices of 4-room flats.
1. **Price Trend (2019 - 2023):** Show the overall trend of 4-room flat resale prices over this period.
2. **Frequent Price Range:** Identify the most common resale price range for 4-room flats (bin size = 100K).
3. **Best Time to Buy:** Analyze which month and quarter are the best times to buy a 4-room flat. Consider whether this trend applies to other flat types.
4. **Floor Range Impact:** Examine how floor levels (low, middle, high) influence the resale prices.

### Dashboard 2 - Location
Examines the impact of location on resale prices.
5. **Most Expensive Locations:** Display towns where resale prices are 1 million SGD and above.
6. **Most Affordable Locations:** Identify towns where resale prices are 400K SGD or below.
7. **Recommendation for Budget:** Suggest a town for a couple with a budget between 600K and 700K, explaining why the town is suitable.
8. **Lease Commencement Year Analysis:** Provide insights on how the lease commencement year affects the resale price.

## Visualization Guidelines
- Use bar charts, line charts, heat maps, or other appropriate chart types to answer each question.
- Make use of filters, legends, and labels to improve clarity.
- Annotate key findings to highlight important insights.
